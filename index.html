Phase 2 · The awakening

Event-driven intelligence that wakes up in under 400 ms

The node spends almost its entire life in deep sleep, drawing only microamperes. A single PIR trigger kicks off a precise four-stage sequence — from photons to classification in under half a second.

💤

Deep sleep

ESP32-WROVER at μA draw. Solar capacitor accumulates charge. Zero processing.

⚡

PIR trigger

Passive infrared sensor detects body-heat motion — wakes the ESP32 in <10 ms.

🧠

Wake → analyse

ESP32 signals Raspberry Pi 4. Mega Detector runs on-device — human or animal in <400 ms.

📡

Mesh alert

44-byte protobuf packet emitted via ESP-NOW. Node returns to deep sleep.
